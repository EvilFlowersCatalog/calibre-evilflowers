EvilFlowers for Calibre

Keeps this Calibre library in sync with a catalog on an EvilFlowers server, so the
same books, metadata, covers and EPUB/PDF/MOBI files are available on every computer
that syncs with that catalog.

* Books new in Calibre are uploaded; entries new in the catalog are downloaded.
* A change on one side is applied to the other. When both sides changed, you choose.
* Nothing is ever deleted, on either side.

From the button's menu you can also:

* Browse catalog: search the catalog, download the books you pick, and manage your shelf.
* Highlights for selected book: see what you highlighted while reading in EvilFlowers.
  Highlights are read-only and come without notes.
* Settings: sign in with your EvilFlowers account, pick a catalog, and choose to
  download only books on your shelf.

What's new in each version: CHANGELOG.md in the project repository.
