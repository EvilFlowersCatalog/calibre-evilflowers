EvilFlowers for Calibre

Keeps this Calibre library in sync with a catalog on an EvilFlowers server, so the
same books, metadata, covers and EPUB/PDF/MOBI files are available on every computer
that syncs with that catalog.

* Books new in Calibre are uploaded; entries new in the catalog are downloaded.
* A change on one side is applied to the other. When both sides changed, you choose.
* Nothing is ever deleted, on either side.

https://github.com/EvilFlowersCatalog
